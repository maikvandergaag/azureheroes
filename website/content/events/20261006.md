Azure Heroes is teaming up with [GitHub Dev Days](https://github.com/scubaninja) for an evening of hands-on learning, community, AI, and plenty of Copilot.

## Event details

- **Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Time:** 17:00–21:00 CEST
- **Location:** [BBTG, Leusden](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bbtg-building-beyond-technology-group/)
- **Topics:** `AI` `Copilot` `GitHub` `Azure` `Community`

[Register now](https://luma.com/k0zfdmr8) or [join us on Meetup](https://lnkd.in/e4GFFYPB).

## Featured speakers

### Rob Bos

**GitHub Copilot Surfaces: My Leveling Up with AI**

GitHub Copilot is everywhere now: inline, in chat, in agent mode, in the CLI, the app, and the cloud. Rob will walk us through his journey of leveling up with Copilot and explain which surfaces matter most at each stage of your workflow.

[View Rob Bos on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bosrob/)

### Paul Stolk

**Stop Prompting and Praying, Start Specifying**

In this hands-on session, Paul will show how Spec-Driven Development can turn Copilot from a hopeful prompt box into a reliable engineering partner, building a full app in just 60 minutes.

[View Paul Stolk on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paul-stolk-62ab442b/)

Join us for an evening of practical learning, community, and AI-driven development with GitHub Copilot at the center of the conversation.
