We're thrilled to announce our next Azure Heroes event — happening at the Microsoft Netherlands HQ! Join us for an inspiring evening of learning, networking, and deep technical sessions from two amazing community leaders.

## Featured speakers

### Soham Dasgupta

**AI Developer Odyssey: Navigating Use Cases, Models, and Technical Patterns**

In the ever-evolving world of AI, choosing the right language model and designing robust applications around it can be overwhelming. Soham takes us through a developer's journey — from model selection and architecture patterns to automating evaluation, testing, and monitoring of AI apps.

### Mike Martin

**1, 2, 3… Testing: Is This Thing On(line)? Meet Your New Microsoft Testing Tools**

Testing isn't just about finding bugs — it's about ensuring reliability, performance, and resilience in your apps. Mike walks us through modern Microsoft testing tools like Chaos Studio, Playwright, and Azure Load Testing.
