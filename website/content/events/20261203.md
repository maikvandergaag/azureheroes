Azure Heroes is teaming up with [Azure Thursday](https://www.azurethursday.com/events/2026-12-03/) for their year-end event at Sopra Steria: a hands-on Azure AI Search workshop followed by a festive pubquiz.

## Featured sessions

### Roelant Dieben (Cloud & AI Architect @ Sopra Steria) & Stuart van der Lee (Lead AI Engineer @ Sopra Steria)

**Building Intelligent Search with Azure AI Search**

In this developer-focused, hands-on workshop, you'll build an intelligent search solution using Azure AI Search, powered by a dataset from The Office (US). You'll go from raw data to AI-enriched discovery: designing an ingestion pipeline, optimising search indexes for relevance and ranking, adding cognitive skills that extract entities and key phrases, and extending the solution with Foundry IQ for deeper reasoning on top of traditional search.

### The Quizmaster (Azure architect by day, quizmaster by night)

**Pubquiz**

Ready for a festive pubquiz? Test your knowledge on all things Azure, mixed with general knowledge, music and more. Grab a drink, put on your thinking cap and let's get quizzing!
