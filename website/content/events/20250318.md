AWS & Azure Meetup at Schuberg Philis — a rare cloud convergence! It's not every day that these two powerhouse communities come together in one place, bridging cloud security, data, and more for both AWS and Azure professionals. But first, we feast.

## Featured speakers

### Jorge

**Choosing Your Headline**

A deep dive into three real-life security incidents where the technical realities and media narratives told very different stories.

### NetApp — Robin Rico, Aymen Selmi & Verron Martina

**Breaking Silos and Unlocking Data Value in a Multi-Cloud Environment**

NetApp, co-organizer of this meetup, shares practical insights on overcoming cloud storage challenges in a multi-cloud environment.

### Sandeep Gangaram Panday

**Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)**

A look at a critical framework designed to navigate increasing geopolitical tensions and ensure operational resilience when working with the cloud.

### Gert-Jan ter Shure

**(Dev)Containers — It Doesn't Only Work on My Environment**

Across IT, data, and software engineering, "it worked on my machine" is a familiar refrain when deploying to new environments or onboarding team members. What if development and runtime environments could be shared across the whole team? That's not magic — that's (dev)containers.
