Get ready for another exciting Azure Heroes Meetup! Whether you're a seasoned Azure professional or just starting your cloud journey, this is the perfect chance to connect with like-minded people, share knowledge, and be inspired by real-world Azure experiences.

## Featured speakers

### Sagar Sharma

**Future of Azure Integration Services**

Azure offers a wide range of tools for message, data, and event-based integrations, and the journey so far has been impressive. Sagar takes us through what's next for Azure Integration Services, with a special focus on the impact of AI.

### Irwin Strachan

**AI Won't Replace You — But Someone Using AI Will**

The age of intelligent cloud operations is here — but are we ready? In this session, we explore how AI is reshaping DevOps in Azure, challenge assumptions, and share real-world experiences from the field.
